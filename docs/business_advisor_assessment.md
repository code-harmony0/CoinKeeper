# CoinKeeper: Core Business Vision & Goal

> **One-Sentence Concept:** CoinKeeper is a personal finance app combining **expense tracking** with **digitized chit fund (rotating savings group) management** to drive savings discipline and financial visibility.

---

## 1. The Core Differentiation (Why CoinKeeper?)
* **Expense Tracker Market:** Highly saturated (Walnut, Money Manager, Wallet). *Low business value (5/10).*
* **Chit Fund/Committee Market:** Completely manual in India/developing markets, run on WhatsApp, paper notebooks, and spreadsheets. *High business value (8/10).*
* **Product Positioning:** Digitizing informal trust-based savings networks and integrating them with personal finance tracking in one place.

---

## 2. Problem & Solution
* **Lack of Visibility:** Digitizes personal tracking ("Where did my money go?").
* **Savings Discipline:** Promotes active goals and accountability.
* **Chit Fund Default/Complexity:** Digitizes rotating savings schedules, due dates, payouts, and automated reminder alerts, eliminating WhatsApp/paper disputes.

---

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* Working professionals, families, small business owners, and community savings groups running informal committees/chits who need transparency without complex spreadsheets.

---

## 4. Key Wealth Principle
* Better money management creates more wealth than higher income alone. Example: saving ₹2,000/month yields ₹24,000/year of real financial improvement.
